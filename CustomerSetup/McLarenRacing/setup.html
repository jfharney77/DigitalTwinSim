McLaren Racing, Woking

McLaren Racing has run on Dell Technologies infrastructure since 2018 and extended the partnership in February 2026, crediting it through back-to-back Formula 1 Constructors' Championships in 2024 and 2025. The named hardware: Dell PowerEdge servers and high-performance computing for aerodynamic CFD and AI, with PowerStore and PowerScale storage for modelling workloads — a Dell AI Factory processing up to 1.5 TB of data per race weekend across the factory, the road, and the trackside garage.

The McLaren Formula 1 team has run its computing on Dell hardware since 2018, and won the teams' championship in 2024 and 2025 with it. At the factory in Woking, England, racks of Dell servers run aerodynamic simulations (CFD — computing how air flows over the car) and AI models; Dell storage systems hold the results. At every race, a portable version of that setup travels with the team: the car streams sensor data to servers in the garage, which trade up to 1.5 terabytes with the factory over a race weekend so engineers can test strategy ideas in near real time.
