US Department of Defense, Fort Zero validation

In April 2025 Dell's Project Fort Zero completed the US Department of Defense's assessment under the DoD Zero Trust Reference Architecture, achieving Target Level validation as a sovereign, on-premises private cloud — tested by withstanding sophisticated attack, not by checklist. The deployment assessed is the unusual customer setup here: not a rack diagram but a decision architecture, in which no network position, device, or credential is ever trusted implicitly. It is the one setup in this collection whose drawing must not contain a perimeter — drawing a wall around it would reverse the claim being validated.

In April 2025 the US Department of Defense finished testing Dell's "Project Fort Zero" security platform and validated it at its "Target Level" — by attacking it with skilled testers and confirming the attacks got nowhere. Fort Zero implements "zero trust": the idea that being inside the network should earn you nothing, and every single access is checked, every time, against seven kinds of evidence. That is why this drawing has no wall around it — in the old model the wall was the security, and the whole point here is that there is no wall to be inside of.
