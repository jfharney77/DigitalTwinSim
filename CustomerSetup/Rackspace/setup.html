Rackspace Technology, hosted private cloud

Rackspace Technology runs managed private clouds for enterprise tenants on VMware Cloud Foundation over Dell VxRail — and pairs the HCI clusters with external PowerStore arrays. In Dell's words, from Eric Miller, Rackspace's VP of private cloud: combining VCF on VxRail with enterprise arrays like PowerStore "is the perfect blend of resources, allowing us to take advantage of HCI's automation, hybrid cloud's agility and enterprise storage's performance and efficiency." That mix — fused HCI nodes where coupling helps, external storage where it doesn't — is the exact argument two of this repo's twins stage against each other.

Rackspace is a company that runs private clouds for other businesses — each customer gets its own slice of managed infrastructure. Rackspace builds these on Dell VxRail: clusters of identical servers whose built-in drives pool together into shared storage ("hyperconverged infrastructure", HCI). For workloads that need more storage than compute, Rackspace attaches separate Dell PowerStore storage systems instead of adding whole servers. A Rackspace executive calls the combination "the perfect blend" — and the trade-off it navigates is the same one two of this repository's models argue about.
