F1Soft, Kathmandu

F1Soft is Nepal's leading fintech group — its platforms carry mobile banking and digital payments for a large share of the country's banks. Dell's customer story has F1Soft deploying PowerFlex software-defined storage in its data centers for performance, agility, scalability, and reliability; its CIO, Akbar Khan, credits PowerFlex with cutting time spent on routine tasks by around 40%. A payments platform is the canonical PowerFlex customer: the estate can never pause, and PowerFlex's whole argument is that growth, failure, and repair all happen while I/O keeps flowing.

F1Soft is a financial-technology company in Nepal whose software runs mobile banking and digital payments for much of the country. Banking systems can never stop, so F1Soft stores its data on Dell PowerFlex — a design that takes the drives inside many ordinary servers and pools them over the network into one shared, self-healing storage system. If a server dies, every surviving server helps rebuild the lost copies at once, while payments keep flowing. F1Soft's technology chief says this cut routine maintenance work by about 40%.
