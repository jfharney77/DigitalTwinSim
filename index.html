DigitalTwinSim

Interactive digital twins of Dell hardware and platforms — each a pure simulation engine behind a data-driven diagram, built to teach one idea per machine. Chips show which twins are running right now; start one with scripts/dev.sh <Twin>, or read the whole thing with no backend at all — scripts/build_site.sh writes it out as plain files (the recipe is docs/STATIC_HOSTING.md in the repository). Real customer deployments assembled from these twins live in the CustomerSetup gallery. To learn the twins in a teaching order, with a question to answer before each one, take the course — or go straight to its Labs track, where each stop hands you a goal and grades the scenario you build. Two engines at once, with an identity asserted across the hand-off, is compose.
